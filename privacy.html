# Privacy Policy — Tiedepalsta Backup **Last updated:** October 9, 2026 Tiedepalsta Backup is a privately operated backup utility used by the administrator of Tiedepalsta.fi. **Google Drive access:** The application accesses the administrator's Google Drive through rclone to upload, list, retrieve, verify, and delete backup files according to the configured retention policy. **Data handling:** Backup files may contain forum database records and user-uploaded files. These backups are stored in the administrator's Google Drive account for disaster recovery purposes. **Data sharing:** The application does not sell Google user data or share Google Drive account information with third parties. Google Drive is used as the backup storage provider. **Data retention:** Database backups are retained according to the configured rotation policy. Uploaded forum files are stored additively and may be deleted manually when no longer needed. **Access revocation:** The administrator can revoke the application's access through Google Account security settings. **Contact:** tiedecloud@gmail.com
